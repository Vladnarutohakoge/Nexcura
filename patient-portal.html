Frequently Asked Questions

How do I prepare for my upcoming hospital appointment?

Fill out the pre-visit Patient Intake Form under Module 6. Please arrive 15 minutes before your time slot and bring a government ID along with your insurance card.

How quickly are lab and diagnostic results posted?

Routine hematology and urinalysis panels are posted within 4–6 hours. Complex imaging and biopsy diagnostics are released within 24–48 hours once verified by the consultant.

Can I reschedule my appointment online?

Yes, open Appointments (Module 2) and select 'Reschedule' next to any upcoming consultation at least 2 hours before the scheduled time.
